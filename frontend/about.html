Our Story

The hearts and hands behind Covers Palette, crafting custom smiles across India.

Made With Infinite Love

Welcome to Covers Palette! We are a boutique crafting workshop dedicated to transforming your precious digital memories into beautiful, physical keepsakes that you can hold close. Based in India, our journey started with a simple belief: gifts should tell a story.

In a digital age where photos are often forgotten inside phone storage, we curate and structure them into physical mobile covers, aesthetic frames, compact ring albums, and elaborate interactive scrapbooks. Every single order is designed individually, incorporating personalized elements, premium cardstock, custom letters, and gorgeous layouts.

Whether you are celebrating an anniversary, a birthday, or just wishing to store your own precious moments, we craft each piece with precision and premium quality materials so they stay durable and beautiful for generations to come.
